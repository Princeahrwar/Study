# Company playbooks — how to use them

Loop structure drifts by org and by year. **Always confirm the actual format with
your recruiter** — they will tell you, and it costs one email. Treat these files
as the centre of mass to prepare toward, not a schedule.

What does not drift is the weighting: each company has a topic where a vague
answer ends the loop. That is what these files capture.

For each: read the "centre of gravity", do the "prepare" list, and rehearse the
"signature questions" out loud. Update the file after every real conversation
with a recruiter or an interviewer — these are living documents, and by your
third loop they should be mostly your own notes rather than mine.
